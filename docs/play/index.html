The page

Paste or type the notes. There is no camera in this app: reading a photograph needs an OCR model that does not exist here, and it is not pretended otherwise. Every card's answer will be a piece of what you paste.

The tutor

It asks about a topic that is in your deck and never states the answer. After 3 attempts it points at the sentence in your notes, because that is where the answer lives. It is not a general tutor: ask about something that is not in your notes and it says so.

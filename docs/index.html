Study

A page of notes into active recall. Every card's answer is a piece of the page, the schedule brings back what you forgot, and the tutor asks about your notes without ever telling you the answer.

Open Study
Status Built and working offline: cards built from a pasted page with every answer verbatim from it; a real SM-2 schedule (the estate's own srs-kit) where a card answered “again” returns in minutes and one answered “easy” in days; a gap finder that names a term the page uses and never defines; a deck export and import that keeps every review date; and a Socratic tutor that only asks about your own deck.
Not built: reading a photograph of your notes. That needs an OCR model, and there is none in this estate — so the page is pasted or typed, and the app says so instead of pretending to have read a photo.

What it does

  • Cuts the page into facts and makes a card for each — a definition (“What is Respiration?”) or a cloze.
  • Keeps every answer verbatim from your page: a card the app invented would be a card you cannot trust.
  • Schedules with SM-2: learning steps first, then the algorithm. Forget a card and it comes back within minutes.
  • Names the gaps: terms you use more than once and never define.
  • Exports the deck as JSON (or Markdown) and imports it back with the review dates intact.
  • Asks Socratic questions about a card in your deck, and after three attempts points at the sentence in your notes.

What it will not do

  • Read a photograph. No OCR, no camera, no vision model — the page is pasted or typed.
  • Invent a card. If no fact can be turned into a card with an answer taken from the text, it makes none and says so.
  • Give you the answer. The tutor's questions are checked against the card's answer before they are shown; a question that would leak it is replaced.
  • Be a general tutor. Ask about something that is not in your notes and it refuses — one job, not two.
  • Use a model, a key or a network. No fetch, no endpoint; CSP connect-src 'none'.

When things go wrong

CaseWhat happens
Bad inputA page with nothing readable returns “nothing readable” and no set is generated from an empty read. An empty tutor turn is refused; a subject with no stated topic is met with a question back.
No networkNothing changes — cards, schedule and tutor are on the device.
Storage full or unavailableThe cards are generated and shown, the deck is not saved, and the app says they will vanish on reload rather than showing a set that looks saved.
Corrupt saved stateReported and offered for export; a new set is never written over it without asking, and a session is never resumed from a partial transcript as if it were whole.

How to run it

  • Here: play/index.html, or install it from your browser's menu.
  • Offline file: open docs/play/index.html straight from disk.
  • Tests: node tests/self-tests.js — the spec's countable “done means” is asserted there.
